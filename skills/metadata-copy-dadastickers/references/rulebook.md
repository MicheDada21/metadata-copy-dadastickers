# Dadastickers rulebook, in brief

Source: [dada21.com/dadastickers/regolamento](https://dada21.com/dadastickers/regolamento). Summarized here, not quoted; the page has the full text.

## Linguistic rules for statements

- 1 to 14 words.
- No personal names, except historical Dadaists. No nations, nationalities or places, except the "Zurigo" bug, kept because Zurich was the cradle of Dada.
- No full stops, semicolons, colons or direct-speech punctuation. Commas are allowed. A single `?` or `!` may close a statement.
- No anglicisms or technological neologisms; where they appear they are deliberate "bugs".

## Collections within the collection

- 81 characters in lowercase, woven into the statements. 33 are frequent (more than three appearances, such as dada, mamma, chi, il clima); 48 are supporting characters (at most three appearances).
- Named series: Dadaist names, Tampoon (a deliberate pseudo-anglicism), invented banks, repeated forms ("uscire il lunedì sera", "Ti ricordi quando avevamo n anni?", "Con il tempo ci si abitua a qualsiasi…"), "Il ricco & il marinaio", "Come non… = storia del…", "Rubare ai… per dare ai…".
- Other bugs: isolated statements that break a rule and belong to no series.

## Rarity

Each statement is worth 5 DADA credits, plus 4 when it contains "dada", plus 2 for three words, 3 for two words and 5 for one word. The statement "Dada" holds the top value.

## This corpus

`data/enunciati.csv` holds more statements than the rulebook's count of cut-outs, and most named series are rare or absent in it. `bun scripts/statements.ts stats` and `characters` give the current figures.
